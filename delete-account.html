QRpybara Account & Data Deletion

Developer: asuzsoftware

Want to delete your QRpybara account?

You can initiate a permanent account and associated data deletion request from this page without reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Click the button below to open an email addressed to our support team.
  2. Enter the Google account email you used with QRpybara so we can identify the account.
  3. Send the request from an email address you can access. We may ask for limited verification if needed to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not work, email asuzsoftware@gmail.com with the subject QRpybara Account Deletion Request.

What will be deleted

After a valid deletion request is completed, QRpybara will delete account data under our control that is associated with the account, including account profile information and other server-side data linked to the QRpybara account, where applicable.

Data stored only on your device

Scan history, generated codes, saved items, settings, or other information stored only locally on your device may need to be deleted using QRpybara's in-app controls, Android app-data settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Purchase and subscription records

Deleting your QRpybara account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop future renewal charges, cancel the subscription separately in Google Play.

Google Play and payment providers may retain transaction or purchase records under their own policies. QRpybara may also retain limited information where required or permitted for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
