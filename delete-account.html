QRpybara Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Developer: Asuz Software

Want to delete your QRpybara account?

You can initiate a permanent account and associated data deletion request from this page without reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Click the button below to open an email addressed to our support team.
  2. State whether you use QRpybara as a Google-connected user or a Guest. If Google-connected, you may provide the Google email used with QRpybara to help us identify the account. Guest users do not need a Google account email.
  3. Send the request from an email address where we can reply. We may request limited information necessary to locate the relevant account or verify that you are authorized to request deletion. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not work, email asuzsoftware@gmail.com with the subject QRpybara Account Deletion Request. Describe the account type and what you want deleted. You do not need to provide a Google sign-in email if you use Guest mode.

What will be deleted

After we receive and verify a valid deletion request, we identify the relevant QRpybara account and process deletion of the account and associated data under our control, where applicable.

For Google-connected accounts, this can include the associated Firebase Authentication account information and a deletion request for subscription-management customer data, where supported by the relevant provider.

For Guest users, local app data can be removed using the in-app account/data deletion controls. If you request deletion by email, we will help determine whether remotely processed account information can be identified and deleted. Additional information may be required to locate a Guest account, but a Google account email is not required.

Scan history, generated QR codes, saved items, notes, settings, and other information stored only on your device cannot be remotely erased by our support team.

Some purchase, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legally required records may be retained where permitted or required by applicable law or under the policies of the relevant service providers. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

If additional information is needed to verify and complete your request, we will explain what is required. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Data stored only on your device

Scan history, generated codes, saved items, settings, and other information stored only on your device are not necessarily accessible to our support team. To remove these records, use QRpybara's in-app deletion/reset controls, your Android app-data settings, or uninstall the app. A remote email request cannot directly erase local-only information from a device to which we have no access.

Purchase and subscription records

Deleting your QRpybara account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop future renewal charges, cancel the subscription separately in Google Play.

Google Play and payment providers may retain transaction or purchase records under their own policies. QRpybara may also retain limited information where required or permitted for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
