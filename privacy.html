Privacy Policy for QRpybara

Last updated: November 4, 2026

QRpybara: QR & Barcode Scanner ("QRpybara", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") is provided by Asuz Software.

For privacy questions, contact: asuzsoftware@gmail.com

1. Information QRpybara May Access

Camera

QRpybara uses your device camera to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera access is used only when scanning is required. QRpybara does not continuously record video in the background.

Photos Selected by You

QRpybara can scan QR codes from images you explicitly select through the Android system photo picker. The app does not require broad access to your entire photo library for this feature.

Location

QRpybara may request foreground location access for specific features.

Approximate location may be used, with your permission, in Product scanning to identify your country and offer more relevant local marketplace results. Location permission is optional for barcode scanning. If you skip or deny it, scanning continues and QRpybara may use the app language or device locale as a fallback market.

Precise location is requested only when you explicitly use features such as "Use My Location" while creating a Coordinates or Location QR code.

QRpybara does not request background location and does not continuously track your location.

QR Codes and Barcodes

QRpybara processes scanned and generated QR codes and barcodes to provide app functionality. Depending on the code type, this may include URLs, product barcodes, Wi-Fi information, contact information, email addresses, coordinates, text, and social profile links.

Most QR creation and history functionality is processed and stored locally on your device.

Wi-Fi Information

When you create or scan a Wi-Fi QR code, QRpybara may process the network name (SSID), security type, and Wi-Fi password. This information is used to display decoded network information and to request connection through Android Wi-Fi system features.

QRpybara does not intentionally send Wi-Fi passwords to analytics or crash-reporting systems.

Contact Information

When creating or scanning a contact QR code, QRpybara may process information you provide, such as full name, phone number, and email address. Contact QR data is used to generate or decode standards-compatible contact information and may be passed to the Android Contacts interface when you choose to add a contact.

Product Information and Online Searches

When you scan a product barcode, QRpybara may send the barcode number or a related product query to third-party product-information services to identify the product and locate available offers.

If you choose to open a merchant, marketplace, or search-engine result, the relevant search or product information may be sent to that external service. Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy policies.

2. Google Sign-In and Account Information

When you choose to connect a Google account, QRpybara uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. The app receives a Firebase account identifier and basic Google account details made available through sign-in, which can include an email address or display name. Your email does not need to be shown in Settings for authentication to work.

When you sign in, your Firebase user identifier can be used as the RevenueCat App User ID to help verify and maintain Premium access. Guest use relies on an anonymous app-specific session identity. Connecting Google does not change which Google Play account owns a store transaction; legitimate purchase recovery may require the in-app Restore Purchases action.

3. Purchases and Premium Access

QRpybara may offer Weekly Premium, Yearly Premium, and Lifetime Premium.

Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play. QRpybara uses RevenueCat to validate and manage access to active subscriptions and Lifetime Premium purchases.

Purchase-management services may process an app user identifier, product purchased, subscription status, entitlement status, and transaction-related identifiers.

QRpybara does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details.

4. Advertising

QRpybara's current Android release does not display third-party advertisements to Free or Premium users. The release does not initialize Google Mobile Ads or request AdMob advertisements, and it does not use Google UMP to collect advertising consent.

QRpybara's in-app Premium subscription and one-time purchase offers are part of the app's own purchase experience, not advertisements served by a third-party advertising network. We do not use the optional analytics and crash-diagnostics controls to authorize advertising. If advertising is introduced in a future release, we will update this policy and applicable consent and Google Play disclosures before enabling it.

5. Local Storage and Retention

QRpybara stores certain app information locally on your device, including settings, scan history, generated QR records, saved items, and related preferences.

Some unsaved or temporary records may automatically expire after approximately 30 days. Items you explicitly save may remain until you delete them, reset the app's local data, or uninstall the app.

6. Optional Analytics, Crash Diagnostics, and Scanner SDK Data

QRpybara offers two separate settings in Settings > Data Processing: Optional Analytics and Optional Crash Diagnostics. Your choices are stored on your device, may be changed independently, and are restored when you reopen the app. These settings control the corresponding optional Firebase data-collection functions; they do not disable information required to provide requested account, purchase, product-search, or scanner services.

Optional Analytics

If you enable Optional Analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase may process usage events and related technical information, such as app interactions, app and device characteristics, operating-system information, and app or installation identifiers, to help us understand and improve the app. If you disable this option, the app applies the corresponding Firebase Analytics collection setting for future collection. Turning it off does not automatically delete data previously received by a service provider.

Optional Crash Diagnostics

If you enable Optional Crash Diagnostics, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash reports and diagnostics including error types, stack traces, app version, device model and operating-system information, timestamps, and Firebase/Crashlytics installation or session identifiers. This information is used to investigate crashes and improve reliability. Changes to automatic Crashlytics reporting, including disabling it, may require restarting QRpybara to take effect. Disabling reporting does not retroactively delete reports previously transmitted. Depending on the SDK's behavior, unsent crash information may be retained locally and transmitted if you later enable reporting.

Barcode Scanning and ML Kit

QRpybara uses barcode/QR scanning components including Google ML Kit. Google states that ML Kit processes scan input such as images and video on the device and does not send that input or the decoded result to its servers as part of ML Kit processing. However, ML Kit may contact Google for technical updates, fixes, or compatibility information and may send API performance and usage metrics to Google for operation, maintenance, improvement, and abuse prevention. These SDK-level operational metrics are distinct from the optional Firebase Analytics and Crash Diagnostics settings.

We do not intentionally include decoded Wi-Fi passwords, complete QR contents, contact-card contents, or precise location coordinates in custom analytics events or crash-report parameters. If you request a product lookup or open an external result, barcode numbers or related search terms may be sent to third-party product-information or marketplace services as described in Section 1.

7. Third-Party Services and Information Sharing

QRpybara does not sell your personal information. Depending on the features you use and the optional settings you enable, information may be processed by the following services:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication: sign-in credentials or tokens, account identifiers, and permitted basic profile details for optional Google account connection and account management.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: purchase-related identifiers, subscription or one-time purchase details, and Premium entitlement status for billing and access verification.
  • Firebase Analytics: optional app usage and technical event information when Optional Analytics is enabled.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: optional crash and device diagnostics when Optional Crash Diagnostics is enabled, subject to the restart behavior described in Section 6.
  • Google ML Kit: technical API usage and performance metrics, and requests for SDK updates or compatibility support. QR/barcode image input is processed on-device by ML Kit.
  • Product-information and marketplace providers: barcode numbers or search terms when you request product identification, product prices, or relevant online results.
  • External services you choose to open: merchants, maps, browsers, email apps, search engines, and supported Android system features, according to the action you select and the external provider's privacy practices.

Service providers may process some data outside your country. Their independent processing and retention obligations may differ from QRpybara's.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can use basic scanning as a Guest without connecting a Google account. Location access is optional for ordinary barcode scanning. You can decline other optional permissions, although features that specifically need them may be limited. You can control Optional Analytics and Optional Crash Diagnostics separately in Settings > Data Processing, subject to the reporting and restart limitations described in Section 6.

You may contact asuzsoftware@gmail.com to request information about data associated with your account, correction where applicable, or account and data deletion. We may request reasonable verification to protect against unauthorized disclosure or deletion. Available rights depend on applicable law.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by QRpybara. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should take appropriate care before creating, saving, copying, or sharing QR codes containing passwords, contact information, locations, or other sensitive information.

10. Account and Data Deletion

QRpybara provides in-app account/data deletion controls for Google-connected and Guest use, according to the relevant account or local-data state. You can also request account and associated data deletion without reinstalling the app through the external page below or by contacting our support address. If you use a Guest session, explain that you are a Guest user in your request; a Google account email is not required. We may need additional non-sensitive information to identify the relevant account or data and verify your authority to request its deletion.

QRpybara Account & Data Deletion

Once a verified deletion request has been completed, account data controlled by QRpybara and associated with that account is deleted, subject to limited retention necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Some purchase and diagnostic records may be retained separately by their respective service providers under their own retention obligations.

Data stored only locally on your device can be removed using QRpybara's deletion/reset controls, Android system settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not itself cancel Google Play purchases or delete every record held by third-party providers.

Deleting a QRpybara account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop future subscription charges, you must also cancel the subscription through Google Play.

11. Children's Privacy

QRpybara is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically designed for children. Users should comply with applicable age requirements for Google Play, purchases, and account services.

12. International Users

QRpybara may be available in multiple countries and languages. Some third-party services may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their own privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when QRpybara's features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
